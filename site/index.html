DropCheck

A free Chrome extension that shows signs that a web shop resells products from AliExpress, Temu or Amazon (dropshipping), and explains each sign. Built for Swedish and Norwegian shops.

What DropCheck does

Click the DropCheck icon on a shop's page. DropCheck reads the page, the shop's own terms, shipping and contact pages and, on Shopify, its product catalog. It then lists every sign it found, says why each one matters, and gives an overall verdict. Nothing is sent to DropCheck or anyone else: the analysis runs in your browser.

How the verdict is reached

Each sign scores points in one of four categories. Every category is capped, so no single kind of evidence can decide the verdict on its own: a young domain plus a missing org. number plus a free mailbox still only count once as weak identity. A shop needs evidence from more than one direction to be called a likely reseller.

CategoryCapSigns
Supply50Product images that keep AliExpress, Alibaba or Amazon file names; Amazon product IDs left by import apps; images that keep the default name of an AI image generator; returns sent to China or an unnamed "international warehouse"; buyers paying import VAT or customs on domestic orders; delivery of ten days or more; dropshipping apps and themes.
Identity35A domain or product catalog less than six months old; no company registration or VAT number; only foreign phone numbers; an email address at another brand's domain; a free-mail address; unfilled template text.
Sales pressure20Countdown timers, "only N left" and "N people viewing" counters.
Pricing20Most products marked down 50 % or more from a struck-through price.
  • Likely reseller - 60 points or more.
  • Mixed signals - 30 to 59 points. Worth a closer look.
  • No reseller signals found - under 30 points.

The platform a shop runs on, its review apps or payment logos never score: they are shown as context only. Wording that limits customs to orders abroad, or a Swedish or Norwegian return address, does not count against a shop.

Questions and answers

How can I tell whether a web shop is dropshipping?

Typical signs are product photos that keep AliExpress, Alibaba or Amazon file names, returns sent to China or to an unnamed international warehouse, buyers told to pay import VAT or customs, delivery times of ten days or more, a newly registered domain or a catalog created in the last few months, no company registration number, a phone number abroad, countdown timers, and nearly every product marked down 50 percent or more. One sign alone proves little; several together are a strong hint.

Does a warning mean the shop is a scam?

No. Dropshipping is legal. A warning means the shop shows signs of reselling products from elsewhere, which often means longer delivery, returns abroad and quality that differs from the photos. DropCheck reports observations about the pages, not a judgment of the company.

Why do file names of product photos matter?

Shops that copy listings from a marketplace often upload the supplier's photos unchanged. The file names keep the marketplace's image IDs, for example a long code that starts with O1CN01 (Alibaba, Taobao, 1688) or an Amazon code ending in _AC_. A shop that photographs its own products has no reason to have such names.

Why would paying import VAT be a warning sign in Sweden?

Sweden is in the EU. A shop that ships from inside the EU never charges Swedish customers import VAT or customs. If a shop that only delivers within Sweden says you pay them, the goods come from outside the EU, usually straight from the supplier.

What data does DropCheck send?

Analysis runs in your browser. For domains other than .se, .dk and .de, only the shop's domain name is sent to rdap.org to look up its registration date. An image URL goes to Google Lens only when you click it under Find the source.

Which shops does it work on?

Any web shop, but best on Shopify, where most dropshipping shops run, because DropCheck can also read the product catalog. Company numbers, addresses and phone codes are recognised for Sweden and Norway; image, policy and pricing signs work for shops in any country.

DropCheck got a shop wrong. What can I do?

Please report it. Shops that DropCheck gets wrong are the most useful data for improving it. Use False alarm for an honest shop that was flagged, or Missed reseller for a reseller that was not.

How do I install it?

Download the zip from the latest release and unzip it. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.

DropCheck is open source: github.com/Hinneman/dropcheck. It reports observations about web pages, not judgments of companies.
