Privacy policy

DropCheck analyses web shops in your browser. It has no server, no account and no analytics, and it never sells or shares data.

DropCheck home

Effective 21 November 2026. This policy covers the DropCheck Chrome extension, version 0.4.0 and later.

What DropCheck reads

  • The page you check. When you click the DropCheck icon, it reads the open page: its text, links, image addresses and page code. With Check shops automatically turned on, it reads every https page you open in the same way, to decide whether it is a shop.
  • The shop's own pages. On a page that looks like a shop, DropCheck also reads up to nine of that shop's own pages (terms, shipping, returns, contact, legal notice and similar) and, on Shopify shops, the public product list at /products.json. These requests go only to the shop itself and are sent without your cookies, so they cannot see your account, cart or orders.

DropCheck does not read what you type into forms, and it does not read pages that are not https. When it fetches a shop's pages itself, it skips account, login, cart and checkout pages.

What DropCheck keeps

  • Derived facts, not page content. From what it reads, DropCheck keeps only the facts it needs for a verdict, such as delivery times, org. numbers found, the number of supplier image names and the shop's domain name. Page text is not stored.
  • Where. In your browser's extension storage, on your computer. Facts about a shop are kept for 24 hours and domain registration dates for 30 days. Verdicts are kept in memory until you close the browser. Older entries are deleted automatically.
  • Your settings. Whether automatic checking is turned on, and the language you chose for DropCheck, if you chose one. The language stays on this computer and is not synced to your Google account.

What DropCheck sends

  • rdap.org: the shop's domain name (for example example.com), to look up when the domain was registered. rdap.org forwards the lookup to the registry for that domain. Nothing else is sent: no page address, no page content, nothing about you. This is not done for .se, .no, .dk and .de domains, whose registries publish no date that shows how long the shop has used the domain.
  • Google Lens: the address of a product image, only when you click that image under "Find the source". Google's own privacy policy applies to that search.
  • The shop: product images are loaded from the shop only when you click "Show product images".

Nothing else leaves your browser. DropCheck has no server of its own and does not collect statistics, crash reports or usage data.

Permissions

  • activeTab and scripting: to read the page you check when you click the icon.
  • Access to https sites (optional): asked for only when you turn on Check shops automatically, so pages can be checked without clicking the icon. Turning the setting off removes the permission again.
  • storage: to keep the facts and settings described above.
  • alarms: to delete expired entries regularly.

Your choices

You can turn automatic checking off in the popup at any time. Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the source code repository, where every change can be followed. Questions can be asked in the repository's issues.
